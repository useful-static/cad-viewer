How do I see a section (cut) view of a STEP model online?

Your file stays on your device. cad.place cuts the model in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Open the model, then press Section in the toolbar (the icon beside Measure's, named where there is room; on a small phone it is in the View menu).
  2. In the bar along the bottom of the view, choose the axis: X, Y or Z.
  3. Move the slider to slide the cut through the model. To put it at an exact place, click the position readout, type a value and press Enter.

Select a part and press ⌖ to put the cut through its middle. Flip shows the other side. A closed body's cut face is filled, so solid material reads as solid. Section × turns the cut off.

You can measure on the cut face, and a view link keeps the section, so someone you send it to sees the same cut.
