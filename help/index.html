Open cad.place

cad.place help

cad.place is a free 3D CAD viewer that runs in your web browser. Your files stay on your device: they are read in the browser and never uploaded. The browser keeps your display choices, the colours you pick for each model and converted copies of STEP, IGES and BREP files on your device, so they open faster; Forget all in the viewer's ? box removes them.
