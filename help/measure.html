How do I measure distances in a STEP assembly in the browser?

Your file stays on your device. Open it in cad.place and measure it there; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Open the model, then press Measure (or the M key).
  2. Choose what to measure in the bar along the bottom of the view:
    • Distance: two points, giving the length and its X, Y and Z parts.
    • Angle: three points, the angle at the middle one.
    • Face: two faces, the first flat, giving the gap between them (or how far from parallel they are).
  3. Click to place each point. Points snap to corners, edge midpoints and circle centres (with the circle's diameter), so a hole's centre is easy to hit.

On a touch screen, tap to place a point, or hold your finger still to aim with a magnifier and lift to place it. A placed end point can be dragged to a new place. The results stay listed until you clear them, and they are drawn in a saved picture.

A file made only of triangles (STL, OBJ) has no corners or circles to snap to, so its points land on the surface.
