How do I check the clearance between two CAD models online?

Your files stay on your device. cad.place compares them in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Open the first model, for example a case.
  2. Choose File › Add model… and open the second, for example the board that goes in it. Both are shown together, and the parts list holds each as its own branch.
  3. If the second model needs moving into place, type how far in the moved by X, Y and Z fields in the info panel (at the top of the left sidebar; where there is no sidebar, open it with ☰ (Parts).
  4. Select the parts to check, and press Clearance in the info panel. It shows the smallest gap between them and the other model's shown parts, and draws it in the view as a distance.

A clearance of 0 means the parts touch or overlap, and the panel names them. The gap is measured on the models' triangles, so it is as exact as the files' tessellation.
