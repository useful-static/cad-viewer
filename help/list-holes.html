How do I list the holes in a STEP part?

Your file stays on your device. cad.place finds the holes in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Open the STEP file and select the part, for example a circuit board or a plate, in the view or the parts list.
  2. In the info panel (at the top of the left sidebar; where there is no sidebar, open it with ☰ Parts), press List holes.

The table lists each hole: its diameter (Ø), its position (the X, Y and Z of its centre) and its depth, with a line per diameter saying how many there are and how far apart. Positions are measured from the model's corner (its lowest X, Y and Z), or from that of the parts you chose with Measure from selection. A through-hole is one row however many faces it crosses, and a countersunk or chamfered hole is one row with its bore and its wider rim.

Holes are found from the circles in the file's faces, so this needs a STEP, IGES or BREP file. A mesh (STL, OBJ) has no circles to find.
