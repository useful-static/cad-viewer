How do I list the holes in a STEP part?

Your file stays on your device. cad.place finds the holes in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

  1. Open the STEP file and select the part, for example a circuit board or a plate, in the view or the parts list.
  2. In the info panel, press List holes.

The table lists each hole: its diameter, its centre measured from a corner of the model (or of the parts you chose with Measure from selection), and its depth. A through-hole is one row however many faces it crosses, and a countersunk or chamfered hole is one row with its bore and its wider rim.

Holes are found from the circles in the file's faces, so this needs a STEP, IGES or BREP file. A mesh (STL, OBJ) has no circles to find.
